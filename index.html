Hello

I'm Mark. I'm a neurodivergent composer, producer and occasional ku poet. I make music for the concert hall, music to be played loudly late at night, and music that is harder to pigeonhole. My latest projects include the chamber opera Little England, Audiobubble, and Fragments of Experience: an Arts Council England funded audio-visual exploration of William Blake's Songs of Experience.
